01 Information we collect

Lap Transcriber is a recording and transcription app. Here is exactly what we collect, and — just as importantly — what we don't.

Audio

While you record, short audio clips ("laps") are sent to our backend and forwarded to our transcription provider to generate a text transcript. The audio is held only in memory for the length of that one request and is never written to disk or any permanent storage, by us or on our servers. Once a transcript is produced, the audio file on your device is also deleted.

Transcripts and session details

The text transcript of each lap, along with session metadata — a title, start and end times, and lap boundaries — is stored in our cloud database, associated with your account, so your history can sync across your devices and survive a phone switch.

Account identifier

You sign in with Sign in with Apple. We request no profile information — not your name, not your email, not even Apple's private relay email. The only thing we store is the opaque, per-app identifier Apple issues for your account, which cannot be used to look up who you are outside our systems.

Device attestation data

To prevent abuse of our shared transcription quota, your device participates in Apple's App Attest program, which proves requests come from a genuine, untampered copy of the app. This exchanges a cryptographic device key and counter with our servers — no personal or device-identifying information beyond what's needed for that verification.

02 How we use information

  • Transcription — audio is sent to our transcription provider solely to produce the text you see in the app.
  • Sync and restore — your transcripts and session details are stored so they appear automatically when you sign in on a new device.
  • Abuse prevention — we use rate limits and device attestation to keep the shared transcription service available and prevent misuse.
  • Product improvement — on a schedule, we export a copy of transcription metadata (language, length, success/failure — never your Apple identifier in readable form) for pattern analysis. See "Data retention" below for how this is protected.

04 Data retention

  • Audio — never persisted; discarded immediately after transcription, both server-side and on your device.
  • Transcripts and sessions — kept until you delete that session, or delete your account.
  • Analytics export — a separate, de-identified copy (your account identifier is one-way hashed, so it can't be reversed back to your account) used only for aggregate pattern analysis, automatically deleted after 12 months.

05 Security

  • All network traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Stored data is encrypted at rest.
  • Your authentication tokens are stored in your device's Keychain, protected by your device's own security, and set to be accessible only while your device is unlocked.
  • Access to production data is limited to the developer operating this service.
  • We maintain rate limits and automated safeguards (including a kill switch) to contain abuse or unexpected cost/usage spikes.

06 Your choices and rights

  • Delete a session — swipe to delete any session from your History at any time.
  • Delete your account and all cloud data — available in Settings. This permanently removes every record associated with your account from our servers and cannot be undone.
  • Sign out — stops syncing; data already stored in the cloud remains until you delete it.

07 Recording other people

Lap Transcriber records audio of whatever conversation you point your device at. Some places — including several U.S. states — legally require the consent of everyone present before a conversation is recorded. The app shows a one-time reminder of this the first time you use it, but it's your responsibility, not ours, to get that consent before you record someone else. This policy covers how we handle data you choose to record; it doesn't grant you a legal right to record anyone.

08 Children's privacy

Lap Transcriber is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us using the details below and we'll remove it.
